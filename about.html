About

Hello, I'm
Mahtab

My journey to UX started at home. As a teenager, I often helped my parents navigate council websites and everyday tasks online. English wasn't their first language, and confusing pages made stressful moments harder.

An internship at After the Flood, a data design agency, showed me that understanding people could be a career. A psychology degree then gave me the tools: noticing what people don't say, and what it means for the services they rely on.

Since then, I've researched public services for the DVSA, DEFRA and the NHS Business Services Authority, from discovery to Beta, most recently exploring where AI can genuinely help people at work. Alongside this, I help independent clients and small businesses with their websites and services.

Talks & Mentoring

Opening doors into UX

I didn't know UX was a career until someone showed me. Now I try to do the same for others.

Each year I return to UCL Careers' Meet the Professionals event to talk with second-year students about careers in user research and design. I've also hosted UCL students at Capgemini's offices for an interactive workshop and hands-on research task.

If you'd like a talk or workshop for your students or team, get in touch.
