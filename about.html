About

Hello, I'm
Mahtab

I'm a user researcher based in London, with a background in psychology. I'm fascinated by how people think, feel and behave, and by what that means for the services they rely on.

Most of my work has been in the public sector, on projects for the DVSA, the NHS Business Services Authority and central government. I've worked from early discovery through to Beta, researching everything from AI tools to developer APIs, alongside designers, developers and policy teams.

Alongside this, I work with independent clients and small businesses on their websites and services, most recently helping a private psychotherapy practice rethink how new clients find and join them.
